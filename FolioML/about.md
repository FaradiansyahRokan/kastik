## 📘 About The Product

FolioML is an AI-powered Knowledge Intelligence Platform designed for professionals, researchers, consultants, and enterprise teams who rely on large volumes of documents, reports, and data to make critical decisions. In a world where information overload has become the norm, FolioML brings order, clarity, and intelligence to the way organizations consume and act on knowledge.

At its core, FolioML is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system — a cutting-edge AI architecture that combines the power of large language models with your organization's own private knowledge base. Unlike general-purpose AI tools that rely solely on publicly available data, FolioML grounds every answer in the documents you provide, ensuring accuracy, relevance, and trustworthiness.

Whether you are a strategy consultant analyzing hundreds of industry reports, a researcher synthesizing academic papers, a legal professional reviewing contracts, or a product team extracting competitive intelligence from market data — FolioML becomes your always-on AI research partner. It reads what you upload, understands the context, remembers relationships between documents, and responds to your questions with precision and depth.

FolioML is built for the modern professional who demands more than a search engine. It is a full-stack knowledge ecosystem — complete with multi-format document ingestion, real-time web intelligence, AI-generated study materials, and a persistent collaborative Notebook system — all wrapped in a clean, intuitive interface that feels as natural as having a conversation.

The result is a platform that doesn't just store your knowledge — it actively works with it, so your team can move faster, think deeper, and decide smarter.

## ❗ Problems We Solve

**Problem 1 — Information Overload**
Professionals today are buried under hundreds of PDFs, reports, articles, and datasets. Finding the right answer inside thousands of pages is inefficient, slow, and mentally exhausting. The result: hours wasted on manual reading instead of high-value thinking.

**Problem 2 — Fragmented Research Workflow**
Research today is scattered across Google, PDFs, spreadsheets, notes, and browser tabs. There is no single intelligent workspace that connects documents, web sources, and AI analysis into one coherent flow — forcing teams to constantly switch contexts and lose critical insights.

**Problem 3 — Generic AI with No Business Context**
General AI tools like ChatGPT do not know your internal documents, your industry nuances, or your specific business context. They hallucinate, generalize, and provide answers that are irrelevant to your specific knowledge base — creating a dangerous trust gap.

**Problem 4 — Knowledge Silos and Poor Collaboration**
Critical knowledge lives inside individual email inboxes, personal hard drives, and isolated notes. When team members leave or change roles, institutional knowledge disappears. Organizations have no scalable system for capturing, sharing, and activating knowledge at scale.

**Problem 5 — Slow, Expensive Analysis Cycles**
Traditional research and analysis processes — hiring research teams, briefing analysts, waiting for reports — are expensive and time-consuming. In fast-moving markets, by the time insights arrive, the window of opportunity may already be closed.

## 💡 Our Solution

FolioML replaces fragmented, manual, and expensive knowledge workflows with a unified AI-native intelligence layer that operates at the speed of thought.

The system works in three powerful layers:

**Ingest:** Upload documents in any format — PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown, CSV, or paste any public URL from the web. FolioML's intelligent ingestion pipeline extracts, structures, and indexes every piece of information into a private knowledge base that belongs exclusively to your account.

**Understand:** FolioML's hybrid Retrieval Engine (combining Semantic Vector Search and BM25 keyword matching) ensures that when you ask a question, the system retrieves the most contextually relevant information — not just keyword matches. Eight specialized AI Agents then take that information and process it through domain-specific reasoning frameworks tailored to your query type.

**Deliver:** Results are streamed back to you in real time — as direct answers, structured study guides, podcast scripts, critical analyses, FAQs, or cross-referenced comparisons between multiple documents — all organized inside a persistent, cloud-synced Notebook that is accessible from any device, anywhere in the world.

Where traditional methods require days of manual work, FolioML delivers comprehensive intelligence in seconds.

## 🔬 Technology Overview

**AI Engine**
FolioML is powered by Google's Gemini — one of the world's most advanced large language model families. Our AI Engine is not a single monolithic model, but a coordinated system of 8 specialized analytical agents that apply distinct reasoning frameworks to your queries. This multi-agent architecture ensures that complex questions receive comprehensive, multi-dimensional answers rather than a single perspective.

**Retrieval Engine**
Our proprietary Hybrid Retrieval Engine combines two industry-leading search methodologies: dense vector search (which understands the meaning and context of language) and BM25 sparse keyword matching (which ensures precision on specific terms and phrases). This dual-layer approach means FolioML finds the right information even when questions are phrased differently from the source documents.

**Analytics Engine**
The Analytics Engine transforms raw document content into structured, actionable intelligence. It powers the AI-generated insights layer — producing Study Guides, FAQs, Podcast scripts, Cross-Reference analyses, and Critical Critiques on demand. Every output is traceable to its source document, maintaining full auditability.

**Database Layer**
FolioML operates on a high-performance, persistent database infrastructure with complete user-level data isolation. Document metadata, embeddings, chat history, and Notebook states are all stored with full persistence — ensuring no data is ever lost between sessions, and all information remains exclusively accessible to the authenticated account owner.

**Security Layer**
Authentication is handled through an institutional-grade identity management system with industry-standard JSON Web Token (JWT) verification on every API request. All user data is completely isolated — no cross-contamination between accounts is architecturally possible.

**Cloud Infrastructure**
FolioML is deployed on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with automatic scaling, geographic redundancy, and zero-downtime deployment pipelines. The frontend is globally distributed via Vercel's edge network, ensuring sub-second load times regardless of user location. The backend operates on Railway's scalable container infrastructure, providing consistent performance under variable load.
